              How to read this report

              Trend tests (out of 8)
              Eight checks of a healthy uptrend: price above its rising 50-, 150- and 200-day averages, those averages stacked in order, at least 30% above the 52-week low, within 25% of the 52-week high, and stronger than most stocks. 7 or 8 means a clean uptrend.
              Relative strength (RS)
              A 1–99 rank of how a stock has performed against all ~900 stocks scanned over 3, 6, 9 and 12 months, weighted toward the latest quarter. 90 means it beat 90% of them.
              RSI
              A 0–100 momentum gauge over 14 days. Above 70 is stretched (overbought); below 30 is washed out (oversold); 50–70 is healthy strength.
              ADX
              Trend strength, regardless of direction. Under 20 is a range; above 25 is a real trend; above 40 is a strong one.
              50-day and 200-day averages
              The average closing price over the last 50 and 200 sessions. Traders treat the 50-day as the medium-term trend and the 200-day as the long-term trend.
              Risk line
              The level where the setup is wrong: the 50-day average or 2.5 times the stock's average daily range below the close, whichever is closer. Not a prediction, just a line to plan around.
              Buy zone vs. Wait
              Buy zone: the trend is intact and the price is not stretched. Wait: the trend is strong but the price has run far above its 50-day or RSI is high, so a pullback offers a better entry.
              Breadth
              The share of stocks above their moving averages. When the index rises but breadth falls, fewer stocks are carrying the market, which tends to make rallies fragile.
              Stance
              Overweight, Neutral or Underweight: whether a sector's technical health and news backdrop argue for more, average or less exposure than its weight in the index.
